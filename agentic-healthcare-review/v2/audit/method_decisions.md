# Decisions and unresolved work

- The focused question replaces the original broad architecture survey. CAA is retained as an analytical checklist, with no claim of being the first safety or authority framework.
- The protocol was frozen after exploratory work; it is not prospective preregistration. Search cutoff is 10 October 2026; retrieval occurred on 11 October UTC.
- Searches produced 2,877 occurrences and 2,397 administrative candidate groups. Exact DOI/title/identifier linkage is not final study-family deduplication. No human screening decisions were entered.
- The 23-report focused map is a provisional purposive subset (16 inherited reports and seven new safety/workflow reports). It is not the inclusion set of a completed systematic review. MedAgents and diagnostic AMIE remain background boundary comparators.
- CarePilot's reported task metric is annotated sequence exact match; it must not be relabeled as an observed clinical transaction endpoint.
- SDC-to-MCP tests a restricted no-execution boundary. Approved dry-run records still execute nothing. Safety claims must preserve this restriction.
- TAO's clinician scenario study involves five doctors and 20 cases; do not label it a prospective patient trial. Its weak reported safety-confidence agreement is retained, not hidden behind headline gains.
- HAARF full reading access was blocked. Its title's use of “standard” does not establish regulatory endorsement. Clinical testing harnesses is a conceptual letter, not an empirical trial.
- PRISMA-trAIce is not used as an endorsed reporting extension. The primary JMIR letter DOI 10.2196/104210 explicitly disputes endorsement. Use PRISMA 2020 and PRISMA-S for eventual completed reporting, with explicit AI assistance disclosure.
- Scopus, Embase, IEEE Xplore and ACM Digital Library searches, librarian PRESS review, independent eligibility/extraction, appropriate design-specific appraisal, clinical adjudication, study-family/version resolution, and author disclosures remain open.
- Public artifacts exclude full reading copies and abstracts. Primary metadata, original notes, search logs, worklists, code and builds are preserved.
