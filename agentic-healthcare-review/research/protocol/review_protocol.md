# Protocol: action-centered critical integrative review
Working title: From Clinical Reasoning to Authorized Action: A Critical Review of Agentic AI in Healthcare and a Clinical Action Assurance Framework.

Scope: LLM-based agents in clinical and administrative healthcare. Modern empirical reports January 2022–10 October 2026; older foundational and reporting literature for context. Include systems with iterative interaction, tools, orchestration, or action proposals and boundary comparators needed to separate dialogue/answer quality from clinical execution. Exclude unrelated robotic control, marketing-only descriptions, and unsupported deployment claims.

Design: purposive critical integrative review and categorical evidence map. This is NOT a systematic review, scoping review, exhaustive census, or meta-analysis. No PRISMA compliance, independent dual screening, consensus extraction, or formal risk-of-bias scoring is claimed. AI-assisted extraction requires independent author verification. Protocol developed during scoping, not preregistered.

Questions: What is the observable action and its destination effect? What authority permits it? What environment and endpoint were evaluated? What evidence supports transition from sandbox performance to real patient care? What control obligations remain at action admission, execution, and recovery?

Selection: 18 empirical reports selected to span collaborative medical reasoning, diagnostic dialogue, image tools, EHR workflows, planning, and administration. Two answer/dialogue comparators are explicitly marked. Selected publications are not a statistical sample. Negative evidence in this selection cannot establish absence across the field.

Extraction fields: persistent source ID; publication/version status; environment; task/sample unit; tools; action/effect; endpoint and denominator; headline result with source locator; access level; interpretation limit. Do not pool incomparable metrics or rank models across tasks.

Discovery: exact PubMed and arXiv queries in scripts/search_literature.py. Database metadata inventory is unassessed; inventory count is not screening count. ID duplicate removal does not establish article-level cross-version deduplication. Search cutoff is fixed; retrieval date is logged separately. Targeted primary-source retrieval supplements discovery.

Recovery note: the original 10 October temporary workspace and uncommitted metadata exports were lost before a repository commit. This rebuild logs new retrievals. Historical counts are not represented as a recovered raw inventory. No missing files or prior visual checks are claimed as current verification.
