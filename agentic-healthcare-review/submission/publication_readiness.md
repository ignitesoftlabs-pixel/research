# Publication readiness
This is a complete, reproducible **research draft package**, not an author-approved submission or a guarantee of publication.

## Completed in this package
- Critical integrative review design and explicit purposive selection limits.
- 18-report evidence map; 54 contextual and empirical references.
- Exact PubMed/arXiv queries; refreshed metadata inventory, retrieval timestamps, identifier-deduplication limits.
- Claim/source register with denominators and interpretation boundaries.
- Five original vector figures, five manuscript tables, formal action-contract/evaluation definitions.
- Main and supplement LaTeX/PDF; build and validation scripts.
- Synthetic reference software and 18 invariant checks, with clinical limits disclosed.

## Required before submission
1. All authors independently verify extracted claims, source versions, access levels, and citation attribution. Clinical and review-method experts should assess evidence and synthesis. Register human checks and corrections; do not represent pending checks as completed.
2. Supply actual author names, affiliations, ORCIDs, corresponding contact, CRediT contributions, conflicts, funding, and author consent. The connected GitHub identity is not assumed to be a manuscript author.
3. Assess novelty against recent broad surveys and safety/governance frameworks. Avoid first-review/first-framework claims. Decide whether a purposive critical review is appropriate for the chosen article type; if a systematic review is wanted, conduct the full protocol, multi-database screening, and human assessment before relabeling.
4. Recheck current journal instructions, AI assistance disclosure, exact format, initial page/abstract/reference/figure limits, and required portal documents. Confirm latest JCR category/quartile and SCIE indexing via authoritative services.
5. Review prose originality, figure rights, public-data/code licensing, accessibility, and final build. Do not redistribute publisher reading copies or patient records.
6. Complete and sign only true originality, simultaneous-submission, ethics, funding, and authorship declarations. The cover letter is unsent and intentionally does not invent these declarations.
7. Obtain final author approval of the manuscript and submission materials. This package performs no external journal submission.
