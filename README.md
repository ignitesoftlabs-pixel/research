# Research projects

## Agentic AI in healthcare review

This repository contains the research process, evidence register, reproducible analysis scripts, IEEE-style LaTeX sources, figures, tables, and compiled PDFs for a critical review of agentic AI in healthcare.

Project directory: [`agentic-healthcare-review/`](agentic-healthcare-review/).

Working title: **From Clinical Reasoning to Authorized Action: A Critical Review of Agentic AI in Healthcare and a Clinical Action Assurance Framework**.

Literature cutoff: 10 October 2026. Work in progress: the manuscript is an AI-assisted research draft requiring author and clinical expert verification before journal submission. No publication acceptance is claimed. No patient-level data, paid publisher PDFs, source abstracts, credentials, or external submission are included.
